TrendsMart Roadmap

Industry-Level Local Commerce OS

Sirf delivery app nahi — qareebi business ka full operating system: order, visit, sit, book, experience. Har category apni business logic ke mutabiq.

Tagline: Har qareebi business — order, visit, sit, book.

1. Vision

Aaj: Hyper-local multi-vendor + WhatsApp orders (delivery-heavy).
Kal: Local Commerce OS — delivery, pickup, sitting, store info, booking, experience — ek hi app.
  • Customer order kare (delivery / pickup)
  • Baith ke khaye (sitting / table QR)
  • Sirf info / discovery (hours, menu, portfolio, map)
  • Experience (cafe, salon booking, repair visit, event / watching)
  • Merchant apni category ke hisaab se modules use kare

Principle: Platform = shared rails. Category = plug-in business pack. Har shop modes on/off kare.

3. Phase 0 — Foundation (pehle yeh)

  • Category themes align (purane Food/Boutique names → naye categories)
  • Modes = source of truth (delivery / pickup / sitting / book…)
  • Service shops auto shop_type=service
  • Sitting: Fast Food ke ilawa Bakery/cafe expand
  • Ek schedule engine (store + delivery + pickup + sitting)
  • Register intent: Products / Food / Services / Venue

4. Universal Rails (har category)

  1. Multi-mode header chips: Delivery · Pickup · Sitting · Book · Open · Distance
  2. Trust: verified, license/NTN optional, hygiene / warranty badges
  3. Digital store passport: shop QR, table QR, deal QR, share card
  4. Live hours brain: channel hours, emergency close, busy/quiet
  5. Order lifecycle: Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready/Out → Done
  6. SME / city analytics (baad mein govt-friendly)
  7. Urdu-first + large fonts + low-bandwidth images
  8. TrendBot: category-aware, real catalog — prices invent nahi
  9. Safety: report shop, Rx path, underage blocks
  10. PWA offline: menu/hours weak signal pe

5. Category Business Packs

A. Daily essentials

Grocery · Fruits · Bakery · Pharmacy

  • Must: kg/dozen units, substitute item, pharmacy Rx upload, freshness, morning-bake slots
  • Modes: Delivery + Pickup; Bakery → light sitting
  • Wow: weekly rashta list, cold-chain badge, pharmacy expiry alerts

B. Food & hospitality

Fast Food & Restaurants (+ later Cafe)

  • Must: full sitting, kitchen KDS, table QR, spice, combos
  • Modes: Delivery + Pickup + Sitting
  • Wow: table wait time, group bill note, hygiene score, festival thali

C. Fashion & lifestyle

Fashion · Beauty · Handmade · Toys · Sports

  • Must: size/color (simple), exchange policy, lookbook, salon/boutique appointment
  • Modes: Pickup + Info + Book; Delivery for ready wear
  • Wow: same-day mall pickup, tailor measurement card

D. Hard goods

Electronics · Home · Books · Automotive · Others

  • Must: warranty, IMEI optional, install add-on, short specs
  • Modes: Delivery + Pickup + Book (install)
  • Wow: genuine-parts badge, nearby repair shop link

E. Home & field services

Maintenance · Security/CCTV · Tech & IT · Personal services

  • Must: job tickets, before/after photos, call-out fee, service radius, emergency slot
  • Modes: Book / Visit primary (plumbing “delivery” nahi)
  • Wow: technician ETA, CCTV survey checklist, salon queue, tutoring recurring

F. New verticals (baad mein)

  • Cafe & Desserts · Events & Banquet · Coaching · Gym
  • Entertainment / Viewing (“watching”: match screening, seat QR)
  • Agri / Dairy micro

“Watching” = Experience mode (sports cafe / hall) — CCTV category alag hai.

6. Award / Govt-level differentiators

  1. SME Digitalization Index (city-wise digital shops)
  2. Structured WhatsApp commerce (status + receipts, card force nahi)
  3. Hyperlocal truth: GPS + radius + channel hours
  4. Multi-modal one shop (lunch sitting, night delivery)
  5. Category OS packs (plumber ≠ pharmacy form)
  6. Citizen safety + verified shops
  7. Youth employment layer (optional later)
  8. Open-hours map for city / tourism
  9. Urdu AI shop assistant (grounded data)
  10. PWA — Play Store ke bina merchant install

8. Monetization

  • Merchant subscription
  • Sponsored ads / spotlight
  • Pack upgrades (Kitchen Pro, Booking Pro)
  • Verified badge (optional, transparent)
  • Product payment escrow abhi force mat karo — pehle compliance

9. Success metrics

  • % shops with 2+ modes
  • Pickup + sitting share vs delivery-only
  • Time-to-first-product (new merchant)
  • Order completion (WA confirm → done)
  • Active SMEs / city; youth merchants
  • Complaint rate / trust score

10. Abhi non-goals

  • Full payment escrow marketplace
  • Nation-wide logistics fleet
  • Social / reels clone
  • Open-domain entertainment AI
Rule: Pehle Local Commerce OS jeeto — phir naye verticals add karo.
