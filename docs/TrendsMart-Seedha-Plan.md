# TrendsMart — Seedha Plan (Asaan Zuban)

Yeh file batati hai ke **hum kya banana chahte hain** aur **baad mein kya-kya add karenge**.  
Mushkil technical words kam, seedha matlab zyada.

---

## Ab kya hai?

Ab TrendsMart zyada tar **delivery / WhatsApp order** wali app jaisi hai.  
Shops, products, deals, cart, pickup, kuch sitting (restaurant) — yeh sab shuru ho chuka hai.

---

## Hum kya banana chahte hain?

Sirf delivery app **nahi**.

Hum chahte hain TrendsMart **har qareebi business** ka app ban jaye:

- Ghar pe **mangwao** (delivery)
- Shop se **khud uthao** (pickup)
- Wahan **baith ke khao / order karo** (sitting)
- Sirf **dekh lo** — timing, menu, address, photos (info)
- **Appointment /call** book k ****aro (plumber, salon, repair)
- Kabhi **event / watching** jaisi cheez (baad mein)

**Seedha jumla:**  
*Jo dukaan paas hai — us se order, visit, baithna, ya booking — sab ek app se.*

---



## Har shop yeh choose kar sake

Har merchant bataye ke uski dukaan kya karti hai:


| Mode       | Seedha matlab                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Delivery   | Ghar pe bhejo                        |
| Pickup     | Counter se lo                        |
| Sitting    | Table pe baith ke order              |
| Info       | Sirf dukaan ki maloomat              |
| Booking    | Time book karo / ghar pe aao         |
| Experience | Baithna, event, watching (baad mein) |


Ek restaurant lunch pe sitting + raat ko delivery — dono chal sakte hain.

---



## Categories alag-alag kaam karti hain

Abhi har category ko ek jaisa treat karna galat hai.  
Har business ki zaroorat alag hoti hai.

### Rozana ki cheezein

**Grocery, Fruits, Bakery, Pharmacy**

- Wazan / dozen (1 kg, 1 dozen)
- Agar item na ho to substitute
- Pharmacy pe prescription photo
- Bakery pe subah ki fresh baking
- Delivery + pickup pehle



### Khana / restaurant

**Fast Food & Restaurants**

- Delivery, pickup, **sitting** (table QR)
- Kitchen board — order ready dikhe
- Spice level, combo meals
- Baad mein: wait time, hygiene badge



### Fashion / beauty

**Clothes, Beauty, Handmade, Toys, Sports**

- Size / color
- Exchange policy clear
- Salon / boutique pe appointment
- Kabhi mall se same-day pickup



### Electronics / ghar ka saman

**Electronics, Home, Books, Cars accessories**

- Warranty likhi ho
- Install / setup book ho (AC, TV)
- Asli parts ka badge (baad mein)
- Pass mein repair shop ka link



### Services (ghar aake kaam)

**Plumber, Electrician, CCTV, Laptop repair, Salon, Tutor**

- Yeh “delivery of pipe” nahi — **booking** hai
- Package price, pehle/baad ki photos
- Call-out charge
- Technician kab aa raha hai (baad mein)



### Baad mein naye business

Cafe, banquet hall, coaching, gym, sports watching cafe, dairy —  
jab pehla system mazboot ho jaye.

---



## Aisi cheezein jo **har** shop ko milengi

1. Shop pe clear buttons: Delivery / Pickup / Sitting / Open abhi
2. Trust: verified dukaan, reviews, sahi timings
3. QR code: dukaan share / print
4. Order status: Pending → Confirm → Ban raha → Ready / Delivered
5. TrendBot: app + dukaan ke sawal — galat price invent nahi
6. Urdu / Roman Urdu asaan
7. Phone pe “Add to Home Screen” (PWA) — Play Store zaroori nahi
8. Galat listing report karne ka tareeqa

## Kab kya karenge (simple timeline)



### Pehle (foundation)

- Categories aur themes theek align  
- Shop modes clear (on/off)  
- Timings ek system  
- Service shops sahi setup



### Phase 1 (thode hafte)

- Shop page pe mode chips  
- Order status live  
- Sitting thori aur shops pe  
- Verified / trust badges shuru



### Phase 2

- Grocery units + substitute  
- Pharmacy prescription  
- Clothes size/color simple  
- Service job tickets  
- Booking (appointment) order type



### Phase 3

- Watching / event / venue  
- Kitchen / tables zyada shops pe smooth  
- “Ab busy hai / free hai” signal



### Phase 4 (bara level)

- JazzCash / Easypaisa (jab ready ho)  
- City / SME numbers (govt / partner ke liye)  
- Naye categories  
- License / deep verification

---



## Paise kaise aayenge (simple)

- Merchant monthly plan  
- Ads / featured spot  
- Extra packs (Kitchen Pro, Booking Pro)  
- Verified badge (optional)

**Abhi zaroori nahi:** platform pe poora payment rokna (escrow).  
Pehle WhatsApp + COD / merchant settlement theek chale.

---



## Success kaise pata chalega?

- Kitni shops delivery ke ilawa pickup/sitting use karti hain  
- Naya merchant kitni jaldi pehla product daalta hai  
- Order confirm se complete tak kitne poore hote hain  
- Shikayat kam, trust zyada

---



## Abhi yeh **nahi** karenge

- Poora Pakistan delivery fleet  
- Instagram/Reels jaisi social app  
- Har sawal ka free AI (sirf TrendsMart / shopping)  
- Bina rules ke online payment hold

**Pehle local commerce mazboot — phir expand.**

---



## Ek line mein irada

> TrendsMart ko itna banao ke har paas wali dukaan — grocery ho ya plumber —  
> customer yahan se **order, pickup, baithna, ya booking** kar sake,  
> aur har category ko **uske business** ke hisaab se tools milen.

Yahi “vast industry” ka seedha matlab hai — mushkil words nahi, **zyada kaam, sahi jagah**.
